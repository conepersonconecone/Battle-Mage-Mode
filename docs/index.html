PKP SYSTEM

Normal
Inventory

    FAILURE STATES

    Overloaded — a restoring attack landed while the bar was already at its maximum, or the gain was more than twice what you were missing. While Overloaded, you cannot attack with Melee or Range or Eat. Casting is the way out. 

    Depleted — the pool went below zero. Drinkables are locked while depleted. It clears by regenerating back to full, or with a Clear Depletion item.

    Critical Overload -  A restoring attack while already overloaded fires critical overload, which strips teleports as well.

    BLOCKING

    Blocking a hit may restore or drain PKP depending on the shield or off-hand equipped.

    Blocking a hit never causes overload, or depletion.

    Dwarf Multicannon

    The Dwarf Multicannon is a window, not an unlock. You may place one while your Ranged is under 45; at 45 the Set-up option is simply gone from the menu, and you are free to keep it as a souvenir or sell/alch it.

    EQUIPMENT

    1. WEAPONS REQUIRE > 0 MAGI ATK
    2. ARMOR REQUIRES > -10 MAGI ATK
    3. NO POWERED STAVES OR AUTOCASTING
    4. FACTION-ALIGNED EQUIPMENT
    5. FACTION-EXCLUSIVE EQUIPMENT
