Battle-Mage Mode

Plugin 2.0.0 · codex format 14 · built 2026-10-22 · 1001 items

GETTING STARTED

Head to the Wise Old Man in Draynor Village. Stand two tiles directly south of him and Ring your Cowbell amulet. Hear him out, choose Begin Battle-Mage Mode, and select an Oath.

PKP SYSTEM

Normal
Inventory

    FAILURE STATES

    Depletion — caused when you deplete too far below zero. Anything you would drink becomes locked while depleted. It clears by regenerating back to full, or with a Clear Depletion item.

    Overload — a restoring attack landed while the bar was already at its maximum, or the gain was more than twice what you were missing. While Overloaded, you cannot attack with Melee/Range or Eat. Cast a spell to exit Overload.

    Critical Overload -  A restoring attack while already Overloaded fires Critical Overload, which essentially forces you to exit combat. The only one-click teleport NOT disable during Critical Overload is the Cowbell Amulet.

    BLOCKING

    Blocking a hit may restore or drain PKP depending on the shield or off-hand equipped. Blocking a hit never causes overload, or depletion.

    For Shields with no defined PKP result, Saradomin faction gains 15 PKP per block and Zamorak faction loses 15 PKP per block.

    Toggle PKP — right-click any shield or off-hand that gains or loses PKP on a block, and choose Toggle PKP to switch its PKP off. Choose it again to switch it back on.

    STATUS EFFECTS

    Alcohol — every sip of an alcoholic drink cuts the PKP you earn from melee, ranged and blocking by 10% for 30 seconds. Sips stack up to 5 (−50%); each sip restarts the timer.

    Poison — spells cost 1.5x PKP while you are poisoned.

    SPELL CASTING

    Combat spells cost 50 PKP and Status Spells cost 25 PKP.

    The elemental spells get cheaper at certain magic level thresholds.

    Some equipment makes certain spells cheaper while worn.

    Autocasting and powered staff attacks are not permitted.

    SPECIAL PKP EFFECTS

    Some equipment adds a PKP buffer while worn: extra room before Overload or Depletion.

    Certain Special attacks, Equipment sets, and Consumables have varying special effects on the PKP System, such as Clearing Failure States, Preventing Failure States, and adjusting PKP costs or gains.

    Dwarf Multicannon

    The Dwarf Multicannon is a window, not an unlock. You may place one while your Ranged is under 45; at 45 the Set-up option is simply gone from the menu, and you are free to keep it as a souvenir or sell/alch it.

    Mage Arena

    Inside Kolodion's arena, every faction may cast Saradomin Strike, Claws of Guthix and Flames of Zamorak and wield the Saradomin, Guthix and Zamorak staff. While Mage Arena II is in progress and the enchanted symbol is in your inventory, the same spells and staves are allowed anywhere in the Wilderness.

    Equipment Ideology

    1. Armour and Weapons are Balanced on a per-faction basis to match both the Battle-Mage Archetype, as well as the god-faction the player has opted into. Each faction starts with a basic rule of +1 Magic Attack for Weapons and -12 or greater Magic Attack for Equipment. Weapons and Equipment exclusive to one faction are often banned from use in other factions.
